---
title: Championship MW2
summary: West Ham lose at home to a Charlton side missing six players. Five draws across the round.
---
**Biggest surprise.** West Ham 1–2 Charlton, and nothing else is close. The
shortest-priced title favourite in the division, 70% at home, beaten by a
Charlton side that arrived without Mbick, Edwards, Ramsay, Kelman and Apter and
still looked like the team that had decided what it wanted from the afternoon.
West Ham spent twenty minutes after the second goal playing like men who had
just been handed a leaflet explaining that the Championship is a real league
with real away teams in it. Two rounds in, they have one point.

**Statement result.** Preston 1–3 Wolves. Parachute money bought a squad built
two divisions up and it showed inside half an hour. Preston are still waiting on
loan signings and it looked exactly like a team waiting on loan signings.

**Worst decision.** Blackburn had gone under 2.5 in nine of their last ten at
Ewood and the last six meetings with Middlesbrough had all gone under, so
naturally Boro turned up, took 77% of the ball, and lost 2–1 to a side who had
three shots that mattered. Possession as an end in itself, in August, away from
home.

**Deserved better.** Swansea 0–0 Sheffield United was a genuinely decent goalless
draw, which is a phrase I'd like on the record as being possible. United are
playing under a winding-up petition and a rumoured twelve-point deduction and
somehow held a back four together for ninety minutes.

**What I got wrong.** Four of eight. The West Ham single was the loud mistake —
70% was too confident, and I'd nudged it *up* on Charlton's absentee list, which
turns out to be a weak signal wearing a convincing suit. The expensive mistake
was quieter: three of the four misses were draws, two of them on matches where
I'd excluded X entirely to fit an 8+0 system. Derby and QPR were both priced at
27% draws. Both drew.
