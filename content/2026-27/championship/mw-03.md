---
title: Championship MW3
summary: Charlton alone with a perfect record, Southampton finally climb out of minus four, and both coupon misses were draws I had left out.
---
**Top of the pile.** Charlton, and only Charlton, on nine points from nine.
Karlan Grant cut inside on 56 minutes and drilled the only goal past Daniel
Iversen. Preston have now lost all three, three days after being beaten 4–0 by
Everton in the cup. Nathan Jones has the division's last perfect record and
the least fashionable squad to have it.

**Biggest turnaround.** Southampton 5–1 Millwall, though the table is the
better story than the scoreline. Millwall had won their opening two without
conceding and led through Sykes' deflected effort. Southampton answered with
five, Larin twice, the second a volley after lifting the ball over Jake
Cooper. It took them to two points and 18th, because they started the season
on minus four: the deduction imposed alongside expulsion from last season's
play-off final after the club admitted filming three opponents' training
sessions, Middlesbrough's among them. Two league wins to reach positive
numbers. A five-goal afternoon that gets you to 18th is a strange sort of
triumph, and an entirely self-inflicted one.

**Worst decision.** The one that wasn't made. Lankshear was standing offside
when Whittaker's shot came back off the post and Max O'Leary's head, and he
nodded it in to put Middlesbrough ahead against West Brom. Boro won 3–1,
Lankshear scored twice and now has six in five since leaving Spurs, and
Albion's unbeaten start died to a goal that should not have stood. Both halves
of that go on the record.

**What I got wrong.** Six of eight on the coupon, and both misses were draws.
Watford–West Ham was an away single that finished 1–1, Nabizada's curled chip
cancelled out when Pollock turned the ball into his own net. Cardiff–Sheffield
United had the draw left out, and three goals in the first seven minutes
became 2–2 by half-time.

**Note for the model.** Two coupons in, four of the six misses are draws, and
every one of them was a draw I had excluded: QPR and Derby in MW2, Watford and
Cardiff here. Four is not proof. It is enough to start measuring instead of
shrugging.
