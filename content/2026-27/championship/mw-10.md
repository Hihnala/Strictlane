---
title: Championship MW10
summary: Lincoln finished with nine men and lost 6–0 to West Ham, Birmingham beat bottom side Preston 4–0, and Middlesbrough won at Stoke in stoppage time.
---
**Biggest score.** Lincoln 0–6 West Ham. Tom Hamer was sent off after three
minutes for a lunge on Joel Veltman, and Conor McGrandles followed on 23 for
pulling back Jarrod Bowen as the last man. West Ham still led only 1–0 at
half-time, through Konstantinos Mavropanos's header from Bowen's corner. Then
Edvin Austbø and Bowen scored within two minutes of the restart, Pablo added a
fourth and Tomáš Souček scored twice late on. Nine against eleven for more
than an hour. The cards explain the scoreline better than West Ham do.

**The run goes on.** Birmingham 4–0 Preston. Liam Millar opened on 33 minutes,
and August Priske scored twice within seconds of each other in first-half
stoppage time. Stan Mills went off for a second yellow early in the second
half, and Demarai Gray made it four. It was Birmingham's first home league win
of the season. Preston are bottom with one win from ten.

**Both ends.** Stoke 2–3 Middlesbrough. Goalless at half-time, then five
goals. Callum Brittain put Boro ahead, Neto Borges turned the ball into his
own net two minutes later, and Will Lankshear restored the lead. Robert
Bozenik equalised on 85, and Borges won it a minute into stoppage time. Same
player, both nets.

**Elsewhere.** Cardiff led Watford 4–0 at half-time and won 5–1, three days
after Watford had lost 4–0 at home to Burnley. QPR beat Derby 4–1, Portsmouth
edged Sheffield United 3–2 and Bolton won 3–1 at Wolves. Burnley and Charlton
drew 2–2, Southampton won 1–0 at Norwich, and Blackburn, Swansea and West Brom
all won away by a single goal.

**No coupon.** A midweek round. Nothing was forecast, so there is nothing to
score.
