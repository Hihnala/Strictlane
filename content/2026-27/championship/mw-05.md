---
title: Championship MW5
summary: Bristol City win at Turf Moor, Stoke are four up on Charlton by half-time, and Preston finally win.
---
**Biggest surprise.** Burnley 1–2 Bristol City. Burnley came down from the
Premier League with the budget to go straight back up, and they are now five
league games without a win. At home to a side that had lost the last eight
league meetings between them and hadn't won at Turf Moor in nearly twenty
years, they managed eight shots and 0.26 xG. Bristol City have won three
straight. Burnley have two points and a parachute.

**Statement result.** Stoke 4–0 Charlton, and it was 4–0 at half-time. Charlton
arrived as the division's last unbeaten side and finished with ten men. Stoke
lost their first three games; they have now won two running.

**Relegated, and suddenly fine.** West Ham 3–0 Derby: twenty shots to four, and
Derby didn't put one on target. Four days after the 4–2 over Wolves, the side
that lost at home to Charlton in August looks like it has worked out which
division it's in.

**Something for Preston.** The MW4 note left them on played four, lost four.
They were 2–0 up at half-time against Blackburn and held on for 2–1. Recorded
here precisely because the last note was so rude about them.

**Also.** Millwall answered eight goals conceded in two games with a 4–0 over
Bolton in which both sides had a man sent off. Norwich won 3–1 at Sheffield
United with nearly four times the home side's xG, and the scoreline flattered
nobody.

**What I got wrong.** Burnley went in as a single, and the reasoning behind it
was the name on the shirt rather than four matches of evidence. It was the loud
miss of the round. The relegated-club trim on West Ham, retired this week after
costing again, gets its obituary from Derby's shot map.
