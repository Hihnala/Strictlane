---
title: Championship MW1
summary: Norwich lose their opener at home to West Brom, QPR win 3-1 away at Portsmouth, and three matches finish level.
---
**Biggest surprise.** Norwich City 1–2 West Bromwich Albion. Preseason
chatter has had Norwich near the top of most people's Championship table for
months, and that survives exactly one opening weekend before a home defeat
to West Brom. One match never decides a season, but it's the one score from
the weekend that doesn't fit the story being told about this division before
a ball was kicked.

**Statement result.** Portsmouth 1–3 Queens Park Rangers. Three goals on the
road on the opening weekend is a loud way to start a season, and QPR didn't
need much fuss to do it.

**Quiet corner.** Three matches finished level — Wolverhampton Wanderers
2–2 Blackburn Rovers, Sheffield United 0–0 Birmingham City, and Cardiff City
1–1 Wrexham — including the round's only goalless draw. Nothing dramatic in
any of them, which is its own kind of information about how the season
opened.

No forecasts were made for this round — nothing here to score.
