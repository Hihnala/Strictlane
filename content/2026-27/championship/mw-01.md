---
title: Championship MW1
summary: Norwich lose their opener at home to West Brom, QPR win 3-1 away at Portsmouth, and four matches finish level.
---
**Biggest surprise.** Norwich City 1–2 West Bromwich Albion. Preseason chatter
has had Norwich near the top of most people's Championship table for months,
and that survives exactly one opening weekend before a home defeat to West
Brom. One match never decides a season, but it's the one score from the
weekend that doesn't fit the story being told about this division before a
ball was kicked.

**Statement result.** Portsmouth 1–3 Queens Park Rangers. Three goals on the
road on the opening weekend is a loud way to start a season, and QPR didn't
need much fuss to do it.

**Quiet corner.** Four matches finished level: Wolves 2–2 Blackburn, Burnley
2–2 West Ham, Cardiff 1–1 Wrexham and Sheffield United 0–0 Birmingham, the
round's only goalless draw. None of them changed much, which is its own kind
of information about how the season opened.

No forecasts were made for this round, so there is nothing here to score.
