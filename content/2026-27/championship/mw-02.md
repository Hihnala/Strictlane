---
title: Championship MW2
summary: West Ham lose again, QPR and Derby both draw with a home single on, and Millwall beat Norwich 3-0.
---
**Biggest surprise.** West Ham 1–2 Charlton. Second week running, the
shortest-priced side in the division found a way to lose at home, this time
to a Charlton side that simply looked more organised for eighty-plus
minutes. A 70% single on the day's biggest favourite is exactly the kind of
call that should hurt more than it costs, and it did both.

**Statement result.** Millwall 3–0 Norwich. No forecast was riding on it,
which made it easier to enjoy: three goals inside ninety minutes against a
side plenty have pencilled in for the top six before a ball was kicked this
season.

**Worst decision.** Queens Park Rangers 0–0 Bolton, backed as a single at
50%, in a match that had the shape of a stalemate from the teamsheets alone.
Ninety minutes of two sides politely declining to commit to anything, and it
went into the coupon as a home banker. That one's on me.

**What I got wrong.** Four misses from eight forecasts, and the pattern
isn't subtle. West Ham, Derby and QPR were all backed as home singles and
none of them won — Derby and QPR both drew, and neither had the draw
covered. Blackburn is the stranger one: marked X2 at 45% away / 28% draw,
and they won it themselves, 2–1 — the one outcome the coupon had explicitly
ruled out. Four matches, four different ways of being wrong, and three of
them share the same shape: a home side backed or dismissed on a number that
didn't hold.

**Note for the model.** Three of four misses touch the same weakness —
short-priced draws and unfancied home sides. That isn't new information;
it's the same failure the draw watch exists to catch. Worth restating until
the coverage actually changes.
