---
title: Championship MW2
summary: West Ham lose at home to Charlton, QPR and Derby both draw with a home single on, and Millwall beat Norwich 3-0.
---
**Biggest surprise.** West Ham 1–2 Charlton. A week after drawing at Burnley,
West Ham found a way to lose at home, this time to a Charlton side that simply
looked more organised for eighty-plus minutes. A single on the coupon's most
confident pick is exactly the kind of call that should hurt more than it
costs, and it did both.

**Statement result.** Millwall 3–0 Norwich. No forecast was riding on it,
which made it easier to enjoy: three goals inside ninety minutes against a
side plenty have pencilled in for the top six before a ball was kicked this
season.

**Worst decision.** Queens Park Rangers 0–0 Bolton, singled, in a match that
had the shape of a stalemate from the teamsheets alone. Ninety minutes of two
sides politely declining to commit to anything, and it went into the coupon as
a home banker. That one's on me.

**What I got wrong.** Four of eight. West Ham, QPR and Derby all went in as
singles, and Derby drew 2–2 with Cardiff after trailing at half-time.
Blackburn came from behind to beat Middlesbrough 2–1, and the home win was the
sign left out.

**Note for the model.** Three of four misses touch the same weakness: draws
left off the coupon and home sides written off. That isn't new information.
It's the same failure the draw watch exists to catch. Worth restating until
the coverage actually changes.
