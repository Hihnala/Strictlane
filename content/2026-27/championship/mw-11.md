---
title: Championship MW11
summary: Birmingham made it three clean-sheet wins in a row, two sides threw away 2–0 half-time leads, and Norwich lost at home for the third match running.
---
**Three in a row.** Birmingham 3–0 QPR. A week after winning at West Brom and
four days after beating Preston 4–0, Birmingham have eight goals in three games
and have conceded none. QPR had scored four against Derby on Wednesday. They
didn't score here.

**Two goals, no win.** Two sides led 2–0 at half-time and came away with a
draw. Cardiff were two up at the Riverside and drew 2–2 with Middlesbrough.
Lincoln were two up at Millwall and drew 2–2. For Lincoln, a point three days
after losing 6–0 with nine men still counts as progress.

**Vardy, then nothing.** Burnley 1–2 Wolves. Jamie Vardy put Burnley ahead on
20 minutes, Fer López equalised before half-time and Tawanda Chirewa won it on
72. Burnley's only league win this season is still the one at Watford.

**Elsewhere.** West Brom were 2–1 down to Blackburn at half-time and won 3–2.
Wrexham beat Preston 4–1, and Watford beat Charlton 2–0 after conceding
nine goals in their previous two games. West Ham won 1–0 at Swansea.

**What I got wrong.** Six of eight. Norwich went in as a single and lost 1–0
at home to Portsmouth, their third home defeat in a row. That run was visible
before the coupon was built, and I still trusted the home side. Southampton
0–0 Bolton was the other miss, with the draw left out.
