---
title: Championship MW6
summary: A midweek round that ended two unbeaten runs, left two clubs still winless in six, and carried no forecast at all.
---
**Two runs, two nights.** Birmingham and Swansea both arrived unbeaten in five
and both left beaten. Southampton put three past Swansea on Tuesday, and
Norwich took Birmingham 2–1 at Carrow Road a night later. Neither run had
looked fragile going in, which is usually how these end. No collapse, just an
away game in midweek.

**Southampton, still climbing.** That 3–1 makes it five without defeat since
the opening day at Watford, and eleven points earned. They are on seven. The
four-point deduction was cleared ten days ago and is already half forgotten,
which is roughly the opposite of what a sporting sanction is for. That is the
EFL's problem to have, not Southampton's.

**Back to earth.** Charlton 0–0 QPR. Ten days ago they held the division's
only perfect record. Since then they have drawn at West Brom, been beaten 4–0
at Stoke and failed to score at home. A goalless draw is a sensible way to
stop the bleeding and not much more.

**Still nothing.** Burnley drew at Wrexham, Cardiff drew with Stoke, and both
are six matches into the season without a win.

**Three that didn't happen.** The Carabao Cup third round took two of them.
Middlesbrough were at Crystal Palace, Millwall hosted Newcastle and Lincoln
were at Bournemouth, all on the Tuesday, so Middlesbrough–Millwall and Bristol
City–Lincoln simply move to 15 September, a week late and nobody much
inconvenienced. Wolves–Portsmouth is the awkward one. Wolves' cup tie at
Everton moved it once, and Liverpool's home Champions League game with
Atlético Madrid moved that tie and this one again, to 20 October, six weeks
adrift of the round it belongs to. Until then, the table for those two clubs
is provisional.

**Note for the model.** Not one of the twelve carried a forecast, because the
coupon is a Saturday product and this round was played on a Tuesday and a
Wednesday. Midweek rounds add fixtures to the record and nothing whatever to
the calibration. The gap widens every time one comes around, and it is better
named than quietly tolerated.
