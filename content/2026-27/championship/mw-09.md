---
title: Championship MW9
summary: Burnley won for the first time this season with four at Watford, ten-man Southampton beat Portsmouth, and Middlesbrough marked their 150th anniversary by going second.
---
**Biggest surprise.** Watford 0–4 Burnley. Burnley arrived bottom, eight games
without a win and 241 days without one away from home. Jacob Bruun Larsen,
Zeki Amdouni and Reo Hatate scored inside 25 minutes, and Marcus Edwards added
the fourth on 87. It was Nicky Hayen's first win as Burnley manager and
Alessio Dionisi's fifth defeat in six. Part of the home end spent the
afternoon chanting at the board. Hard to argue with them.

**Derby day.** Southampton 1–0 Portsmouth. James Bree scored on 20 minutes
from Finn Azaz's pass, Cameron Bragg went off for a second yellow a minute
before half-time, and Southampton held on with ten for the whole second half.
Their first league win over Portsmouth since 2004 came in the week Tonda
Eckert was handed a suspended six-week ban over the spying affair.

**Relief.** Sheffield United 3–0 Lincoln. Patrick Bamford scored twice, either
side of a Tahith Chong goal, and a three-match losing run was over. Chris
Wilder admitted the pressure had cranked up.

**A birthday win.** Middlesbrough 1–0 Wolves. The club's 150th anniversary
fixture, sold out, was settled by Will Lankshear's header from Tommy Conway's
cross on 57 minutes. Boro moved above West Ham into second, two points behind
Swansea.

**Elsewhere.** Birmingham won 1–0 at West Brom, Swansea came from behind to
beat Norwich 2–1, and Stoke won 2–1 at Bolton. West Ham trailed QPR at
half-time and drew 1–1.

**What I got wrong.** Four of seven. Two of the misses had the home win left
out: Sheffield United beat Lincoln and Middlesbrough beat Wolves. Lincoln had
won three of their previous four away games, which explains the call without
excusing it. Watford–Burnley was the third. A bottom side eight games without
a win looked like an easy exclusion, and the away win was the one sign I
didn't play.
