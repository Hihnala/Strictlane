title: League One MW1
summary: Leicester draw at Notts County on the opening day, Reading and Luton share seven goals, and two matches finish level.
---
**Biggest surprise.** Notts County 1–1 Leicester City. One of the sides most
fancied for an immediate promotion push dropped two points on the opening
day — a draw that will look fine in isolation and considerably less fine if
it turns out to be the shape of the season.

**Statement result.** Reading 3–4 Luton Town. Seven goals across ninety
minutes, and a losing side that put three past the opposition and still went
home with nothing. Huddersfield Town's 3–0 win over AFC Wimbledon was the
tidier way to make a point; Reading and Luton did it the loud way.

**Quiet corner.** Two matches finished level — Blackpool 1–1 Wycombe
Wanderers and Burton Albion 1–1 Stevenage — the only two sides in the
division who didn't leave opening day with an answer either way.

No forecasts were made for this round — nothing here to score.
