---
title: League One MW1
summary: Luton come from 3–1 down to win at Reading, Leicester draw at Notts County, and four matches finish level.
---
**Biggest comeback.** Reading 3–4 Luton. Jake Richards put Luton ahead after
six minutes, and Jack Marriott answered with a hat-trick inside half an hour.
Reading led 3–1 at half-time. Kasey Palmer scored twice in the first seven
minutes of the second half, and Gideon Kodua won it on 90. A hat-trick and a
home defeat on the same afternoon takes some doing.

**Leicester start slowly.** Notts County 1–1 Leicester. Jayden Joseph put
Leicester ahead on 62 minutes and Conor Grant equalised nine minutes from time,
in front of 17,172 at Meadow Lane. A point away at a decent side is no
disaster. It is also not how a club with Leicester's budget wants to open its
first third-tier season since 2008–09.

**Elsewhere.** Huddersfield beat AFC Wimbledon 3–0, Stockport won 3–1 at
Plymouth, and Sheffield Wednesday came from 1–1 at half-time to win 2–1 at
Leyton Orient. Bromley won 1–0 at Barnsley. Cambridge led Wigan 2–0 at the
break and held on for 3–2. Oxford trailed MK Dons at half-time and drew 2–2,
and Blackpool–Wycombe and Burton–Stevenage both finished 1–1.

**No coupon.** No forecasts were made for this round, so there is nothing to
score.
