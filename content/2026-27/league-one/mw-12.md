---
title: League One MW12
summary: Stockport and Huddersfield share the top on 22 points while Plymouth lose again and ten-man Sheffield Wednesday fall at Notts County.
---
**Two clubs on 22.** Doncaster 0–2 Stockport. Mamadou Jobe scored on 53
minutes and Kyle Wootton six minutes later. Stockport had 17 shots to
Doncaster's four, and that's four league wins in a row. Huddersfield 4–1
Peterborough. Ilias Bronkhorst scored after three minutes, Joe Taylor got two
in four minutes after the break and Bali Mumba added a fourth on 79. Both
clubs have 22 points and a goal difference of 12. Stockport go top on goals
scored. Bradford drew 0–0 at Burton and are third on 21, with ten goals from
eleven games.

**Plymouth keep sliding.** Stevenage 3–0 Plymouth. Michael Mellon scored on 19
minutes and Josh Magennis on 27. Phoenix Patterson added a third on 83.
Plymouth were top going into Saturday's round. Two defeats later they are
fifth, with five goals conceded and none scored.

**Three days after five.** Notts County 2–0 Sheffield Wednesday. Daniel Kanu
scored a penalty on 38 minutes. Jamal Lowe was sent off on 50, and Darius
Lipsiuc made it 2–0 on 81. Wednesday had beaten AFC Wimbledon 5–0 on Saturday.
Notts County had gone six league games without a win, the last four of them
defeats. Their previous win came on 29 August.

**Elsewhere.** Leicester were 3–0 up at Bromley by half-time and won 4–0.
Cambridge trailed Wycombe by two goals after Jayden Wareham's second on 61
minutes and drew 2–2. Shayne Lavery scored on 78, and Wycombe's Connor Taylor
scored an own goal in stoppage time. Cambridge have lost once in eleven league
games. Mansfield won 3–0 at AFC Wimbledon, who have now gone five league games
without a win. Blackpool beat MK Dons 2–0 to end a run of six without a win.
Luton beat Barnsley 2–1, Barnsley's third defeat in a row. Oxford beat Wigan
2–1 and have lost once in nine. Reading beat Leyton Orient 1–0 on Wednesday.

**No coupon.** A midweek round. Nothing was forecast, so there is nothing to
score.
