---
title: League One MW4
summary: Bromley concede twelve in two games, Leicester win for the first time, and Mansfield and Luton both swing by five goals in a week.
---
**Twelve conceded in two.** Bromley 0–5 Leyton Orient, three days after
conceding seven at Hillsborough. Their first two defeats of the season have
cost them twelve goals for two scored.

**First win.** Leicester 2–0 Plymouth, both goals before half-time. After a
draw at Notts County, a home defeat to Burton and a goalless draw at MK Dons,
it was overdue. Beating Plymouth at home proves little, but this squad needed
to stop looking lost more than it needed to look good.

**Both ends of five.** Mansfield beat Luton 5–2 on the Sunday and lost 5–0 at
Reading on the Wednesday, three down by half-time. Luton answered their 5–2
by beating Stockport 2–0, four days after Stockport had scored five against
Wycombe. Everybody in this division is currently capable of everything.

**Elsewhere.** Sheffield Wednesday won 2–1 at Wycombe, and Huddersfield beat
Oxford 3–1 after a goalless first half. Cambridge won 2–1 at Bradford, Burton
beat AFC Wimbledon 4–1, Wigan beat MK Dons 3–1 and Barnsley beat Blackpool
2–1. Only Doncaster–Notts County (1–1) and Peterborough–Stevenage (0–0) failed
to produce a winner. The round produced 38 goals, and after four rounds the
division is averaging just over three a game.

**No coupon.** A midweek round. Nothing was forecast, so there is nothing to
score.
