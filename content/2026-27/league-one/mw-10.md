title: League One MW10
summary: Wednesday rescue a point at Huddersfield in the 98th minute, Peterborough lead 3–0 at Leicester by half-time, and Reading score five at MK Dons.
---
**Eight minutes of stoppage time.** Huddersfield 2–2 Sheffield Wednesday. Joe
Taylor scored a minute after the restart and Derensili Sanches Fernandes made
it 2–0 on 68 minutes. Ibane Bowat turned the ball into his own net two
minutes from time, and Jamal Lowe equalised from the penalty spot in the
eighth minute of stoppage time. 19,794 watched a game that looked finished
twice.

**Biggest surprise.** Leicester 1–3 Peterborough. Owura Edwards scored after 12
minutes, Harry Leonard added a second and Warren O'Hora's own goal made it 3–0
before half-time. Emil Riis pulled one back. Peterborough arrived second from
bottom with one win all season, and nearly 30,000 at the King Power saw them
win easily. Leicester have won two of eight.

**Five at MK.** MK Dons 1–5 Reading. Paddy Lane scored before half-time, an own
goal from Bramall made it two, and Kyreece Lisbie scored twice. MK Dons are
still waiting for their first league win.

**Elsewhere.** Luton won 4–1 at Wycombe and led 3–0 within a minute of the
restart. Stockport trailed Barnsley at half-time and won 3–1, Kyle Wootton
scoring twice. Plymouth beat AFC Wimbledon 2–1 with a stoppage-time winner
from Xavier Amaechi and stay top. Oxford won 2–1 at Notts County, and Bradford
drew 0–0 with Leyton Orient.

**What I got wrong.** One League One row and one miss. Huddersfield–Wednesday
went in with the draw left out, and it finished level in the 98th minute. That
is the worst way to lose a row, but the draw was the sign I chose not to
cover.
