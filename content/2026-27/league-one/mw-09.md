---
title: League One MW9
summary: Nine more postponements, Plymouth win at Orient to go top on goal difference, and Huddersfield score three at Burton.
---
**Three games, and a new leader.** Nine of the twelve fixtures were postponed,
so the table moved on the strength of three results. Leyton Orient 0–2
Plymouth was the one that mattered. After a goalless first half, Aribim Pepple
scored on 52 minutes and Will Evans four minutes later. Plymouth are top on 16
points, level with Bradford and ahead on goal difference.

**Bradford held.** Reading 1–1 Bradford. Jake Beesley scored for Bradford after
four minutes, and Udoka Godwin-Malife equalised midway through the second
half. A point away at a side that has scored freely is a fair result, though
it cost Bradford first place.

**Huddersfield back to winning.** Burton 1–3 Huddersfield. Derensili Sanches
Fernandes and Bali Mumba scored either side of Finn Delap's goal for Burton,
and Ashley Fletcher added the third on 69 minutes. A fortnight after losing at
Bromley, Huddersfield are third.

**No coupon.** No League One fixture was on the coupon, so there is nothing to
score.
