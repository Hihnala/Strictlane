---
title: League One MW5
summary: Oxford win 4–0 at Leicester, Stevenage score four second-half goals at Barnsley, and Sheffield Wednesday stay top.
---
**Biggest surprise.** Leicester 0–4 Oxford. Ten years after winning the Premier
League, Leicester lost 4–0 at home in the third tier to the club that came down
with them, managed by a man in his first permanent job. They were home
favourites, had just won their first game of the season, and were beaten on
every measure that matters: 2.31 xG to 1.03, one down at half-time, four by the
end.

**Say when it was luck.** Barnsley 1–5 Stevenage was 1–1 at half-time, then
Stevenage scored four. The xG was nearly level, 1.93 to 1.83. That isn't a
hammering on the underlying numbers; it's a second half in which everything
Stevenage hit went in. Barnsley shouldn't read much into it, and neither should
anyone pricing Stevenage next week.

**Top, and not entirely convincing.** Sheffield Wednesday won 2–1 at
Peterborough with 0.96 xG against 2.25 and stay top. A lead built on that kind
of afternoon needs watching, not celebrating.

**Blown.** Wycombe led MK Dons 2–0 at half-time and drew 3–3. They are still
without a win. MK Dons have now drawn four of five, which is a talent of sorts.

**Also.** Stockport were 3–0 up at Wigan by the break and won 4–0; Wigan are
bottom. Reading beat Blackpool 3–1 on 3.5 xG to 0.51 and will have to wait for
their game in hand, after the rearranged trip to Oxford was called off for the
Carabao Cup. Luton had 23 shots to Leyton Orient's seven and drew 1–1.

No coupon fixtures this week, so no forecasts to answer for.
