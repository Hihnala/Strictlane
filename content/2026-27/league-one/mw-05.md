---
title: League One MW5
summary: Oxford win 4–0 at Leicester, Stevenage score with every shot on target at Barnsley, and Sheffield Wednesday go top.
---
**Biggest surprise.** Leicester 0–4 Oxford. Mark Harris scored after eight
minutes, and Oxford added three more in twenty minutes of the second half,
Harris again among them. Leicester actually had more shots, 14 to 12, but
Oxford put six on target and scored four of them. Ten years after winning the
Premier League, and four days after their first win of the season, Leicester
lost by four at home in the third tier.

**Five from five.** Barnsley 1–5 Stevenage. Cameron McGeehan's penalty and
Oliver Sanderson's reply made it 1–1 at half-time. Daniel Kemp then scored
twice, Terence VanCooten once, and Michael Mellon added a fifth in stoppage
time. Stevenage had five shots on target and scored with all of them. Barnsley
had 17 shots, nine of them blocked. Nobody should read much into it, Stevenage
included.

**Top, without much to spare.** Peterborough 1–2 Sheffield Wednesday.
Peterborough had 20 shots to Wednesday's 13 but put only three on target. A
Josh Feeney own goal and Yan Valery's winner on 79 minutes took Wednesday top,
a point ahead of Huddersfield.

**Blown.** Wycombe led MK Dons 2–0 at half-time and drew 3–3. Wycombe are still
without a win, and MK Dons have now drawn four of five.

**Elsewhere.** Stockport were 3–0 up at Wigan by the break and won 4–0, and
Wigan are bottom. Reading beat Blackpool 3–1 with six shots on target to one.
Luton had 23 shots to Leyton Orient's seven and drew 1–1. Plymouth won 3–2 at
Doncaster, and AFC Wimbledon won 2–0 at Bromley on the Monday.

**No coupon.** No League One fixture was on the coupon, so there is nothing to
score.
