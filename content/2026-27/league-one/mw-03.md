title: League One MW3
summary: Sheffield Wednesday put seven past Bromley in a 43-goal round, Leicester are still without a win, and both League One coupon rows landed.
---
**Seven.** Sheffield Wednesday 7–2 Bromley. Callum Slattery scored after seven
minutes, Mason Burstow made it two and Jamal Lowe a third on the stroke of
half-time. Louie Barry scored twice within four minutes of the restart, Lowe
added his second, and Gabriel Otegbayo scored at both ends. Wednesday had 29
shots to Bromley's seven, 15 of them on target. It was Bromley's first defeat
of the season.

**43 goals in eleven games.** Mansfield led Luton 3–0 at half-time and won 5–2,
Blackpool beat Peterborough 4–0, and Stockport beat Wycombe 5–1. Just under
four goals a game. Nothing about this division is cagey yet.

**Still waiting.** MK Dons 0–0 Leicester. Three games, two points and no win.

**Moved.** Oxford–Reading did not take place. Thames Valley Police asked for it
to be moved because of other events over the bank holiday weekend, and after a
second change it is now set for 4 November.

**What I got wrong.** Nothing. Huddersfield won 2–1 at Cambridge with both
sides' goals coming after half-time, and Stockport beat Wycombe 5–1. Two rows
from two is a sample of two.
