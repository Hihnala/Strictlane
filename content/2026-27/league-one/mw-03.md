---
title: League One MW3
summary: Sheffield Wednesday put seven past Bromley in a 43-goal weekend, and both League One coupon marks landed.
---
**Biggest surprise.** Not that Wednesday won, but by how much and how
completely. Sheffield Wednesday 7–2 Bromley, from a match Wednesday finished
with 29 shots to 7 and 15 on target to 2. Slattery scored in the seventh
minute, Burstow made it two, Lowe added a third on the stroke of half-time, and
Louie Barry scored twice more after the break. Lowe alone had nine shots.
Bromley's first defeat in the division, and a fairly firm answer to whatever
they wanted to know about the third tier.

**The weekend's shape.** Mansfield put five past Luton, Blackpool four past
Peterborough, Stockport five past Wycombe. Eleven matches produced 43 goals,
just under four a game. Whatever else this division is, it is not cagey yet.

**The quiet one.** MK Dons 0–0 Leicester. Leicester's first outing at this
level in a generation is going about as expected: no goals, no rhythm, and a
point that nobody watching enjoyed.

**Still pending.** Oxford United–Reading did not take place and is rescheduled
for 8 September. No score, no placeholder — it sits in the record as unplayed,
which is what it is.

**What I got wrong.** Nothing, for once. Cambridge–Huddersfield went the away
side's way at 40%, and Stockport's 45% turned into 5–1. Two from two is two
matches. It is not evidence of anything.
