**Biggest surprise.** Leicester City 1–2 Burton Albion. Whatever life in the
third tier is supposed to look like for a promotion favourite, it isn't
usually losing at home to Burton. This is the kind of result a side with
Leicester's expectations doesn't get to have more than once.

**Statement result.** Wigan Athletic 0–2 Leyton Orient. Away from home, no
fuss, two goals and the points — the sort of result that says more about a
season's early trajectory than anything in the table this far out.

**What I got wrong.** Only one League One match carried a forecast this
round — Wycombe 1–1 Plymouth, backed 1-or-2 at 38/35 with the draw
excluded — and the draw is exactly what showed up. One miss from one call
isn't a pattern yet; it's a coin that landed on the side that wasn't picked.

**Quiet corner.** Two goalless draws on the same afternoon — Doncaster 0–0
Barnsley and AFC Wimbledon 0–0 Reading — plus three separate 1–1 finishes
(Luton–Notts County, Bromley–Cambridge, MK Dons–Huddersfield). A round with
almost nothing forecast still managed to be a round with almost nothing
decided: five of twelve matches finished level.

**Note for the model.** League One barely gets forecast at all right now,
and the one match that did carry a call missed on a draw — the same shape as
the Championship weakness above, on a much smaller sample. Not enough to act
on yet; worth watching before it turns from coincidence into a pattern.
