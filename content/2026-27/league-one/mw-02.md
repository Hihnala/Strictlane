title: League One MW2
summary: Bradford win at Hillsborough, Leicester lose at home to Burton, half the round finishes level, and the one League One row misses on a draw.
---
**Thursday night at Hillsborough.** Sheffield Wednesday 0–1 Bradford. The
round opened two days early, and Bradford left with a second win from two and
a clean sheet against the side that would top the table a fortnight later.

**Biggest surprise.** Leicester 1–2 Burton. All three goals came before
half-time, and Leicester could not find a way back in the second. That is one
point from two games, and a home defeat to Burton is not on the list of things
a club this size expects from its first month in League One.

**Away and clean.** Wigan 0–2 Leyton Orient, and Blackpool led 2–0 at Stockport
by half-time before winning 2–1.

**Half the round level.** Six of the twelve finished as draws. Doncaster–
Barnsley and AFC Wimbledon–Reading were goalless, Luton and Notts County drew
2–2, and Bromley–Cambridge, MK Dons–Huddersfield and Wycombe–Plymouth all
ended 1–1.

**What I got wrong.** One League One row, and it missed. Wycombe–Plymouth went
in with the draw left out, Plymouth led at half-time, and it finished 1–1. One
miss from one row is not a pattern. It goes on the record all the same.
