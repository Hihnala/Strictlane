---
title: League One MW11
summary: Huddersfield win at Plymouth, Bradford go back to the top, one point now covers five clubs, MK Dons finally win, and Wednesday score five.
---
**One point covers five.** Plymouth 0–2 Huddersfield. Derensili Sanches
Fernandes scored after six minutes, and Huddersfield added a second after the
break to knock the leaders off the top. Bradford beat Stevenage 2–0 and go
back to first on 20 points. Behind them Stockport, Huddersfield, Plymouth and
Cambridge all have 19, and Sheffield Wednesday 18. Eleven rounds in, six clubs
are separated by two points.

**Five without reply.** Sheffield Wednesday 5–0 AFC Wimbledon. Wednesday were
3–0 up at half-time. Wimbledon have now gone four league games without a win
and conceded seven in the last two.

**At last.** MK Dons 2–1 Luton. Alex Gilbey scored on 25 minutes, Jordan Clark
equalised, and Lewis Collins won it from the penalty spot on 79 minutes. It is
MK Dons' first league win of the season, at the ninth attempt. Luton, who won
4–1 at Wycombe a week ago, lost again.

**Elsewhere.** Cambridge trailed at Barnsley at half-time and won 2–1, Ben
Knight equalising and Shayne Lavery scoring the winner in stoppage time.
Stockport beat Bromley 1–0 for a third straight win. Wigan beat Reading 1–0,
a week after Reading scored five at MK. Blackpool lost 2–0 at Leyton Orient
and have gone six league games without a win. Leicester beat Doncaster 2–1,
and Mansfield and Oxford drew 1–1 on Thursday night.

**What I got wrong.** Nothing. One League One row, Wednesday at home to
Wimbledon, and it landed by five goals. One row says nothing about anything.
