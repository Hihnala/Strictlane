---
title: League One MW7
summary: Sheffield Wednesday's third straight draw, Plymouth's third straight win, and Blackpool's four-match slide continue.
---
**Not every stalemate is a missed chance.** Sheffield Wednesday 0–0 Stockport
County. Three weeks after putting seven past Bromley, Wednesday have now drawn
three league games running at Hillsborough, missing Louie Barry — the double
scorer that day — to injury throughout. Stockport, unbeaten in six, matched
the league leaders in front of more than 32,000 and were arguably the better
side in the first half. Two decent sides cancelling each other out is a
different thing from either side having an off day, and this was the former.

**Statement result.** Plymouth won 4–2 at Blackpool, a third straight victory
built on two goals from Oliver Irow either side of half-time, either side of
one from Jerry Yates. Blackpool's bright start to the season is now a
four-match run without a win, and the finishing touch has deserted both ends
of the pitch across it.

**Elsewhere.** Reading beat Notts County 2–0, Leicester's tricky return to the
third tier continued with a draw at Barnsley, and MK Dons' wait for a first
league win goes on after a goalless draw at AFC Wimbledon that satisfied
nobody involved.

**Worth tracking.** Bradford won again at Luton, extending a run that is
starting to look less like a run of upsets and more like the level the club is
actually playing at this season. Worth treating them as a genuine promotion
contender before the table forces the issue rather than after.
