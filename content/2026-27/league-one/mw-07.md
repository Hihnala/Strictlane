title: League One MW7
summary: Sheffield Wednesday's second straight goalless draw at home, Plymouth's third straight win, Bradford go top, and all three coupon rows landed.
---
**Two goalless home games running.** Sheffield Wednesday 0–0 Stockport
County. Three weeks after putting seven past Bromley, with Louie Barry scoring
twice, Wednesday have now drawn two league games in a row at Hillsborough
without scoring, Wigan first and Stockport now. 32,290 watched it. Two decent
sides cancelled each other out, which is a different thing from either side
having an off day.

**Statement result.** Blackpool 2–4 Plymouth. Jordan Brown put Blackpool
ahead, and Oliver Irow scored either side of half-time to turn it round.
Harvey White added a third, Jerry Yates pulled one back on 70 minutes, and
Aribim Pepple settled it a minute from time. That is three straight wins for
Plymouth. Blackpool have now gone four league games without a win.

**Bradford go top.** Bobby Pointon scored after four minutes at Kenilworth
Road, and Luton's Harrison Ashby was sent off deep in stoppage time. It is
Bradford's third straight 1–0 win, and with Wednesday drawing they lead the
division. At some point a run of narrow wins stops being luck. Treat them as a
promotion side now rather than when the table forces it.

**Elsewhere.** Leicester led at Barnsley through Liam Cullen after seven
minutes and played against ten from the 24th, when Luca Connell was sent off.
Reyes Cleary still equalised just after the hour. Bromley beat Huddersfield 2–1
with goals from JJ McKiernan and Mikel Miller, and Oxford and Cambridge drew
1–1. Reading beat Notts County 2–0, Doncaster won 2–0 at Peterborough, and MK
Dons' wait for a first league win goes on after a goalless draw at AFC
Wimbledon.

**What I got wrong.** Nothing. Three League One rows, three hits: Oxford's
draw, Bradford's win at Luton and Wednesday's draw with Stockport. Three rows
is far too small a sample to mean anything beyond this Saturday.
