---
title: League One MW8
summary: Eight of twelve fixtures postponed, Burton end Plymouth's winning run at Home Park, and Danny Ings rescues a point for Wycombe in the 90th minute.
---
**A third of a round.** Only four of the twelve fixtures went ahead. The rest
were postponed, Leicester's home game with Wigan among them after at least
three of their players were called up for international duty. League One plays
through international windows, but a club can ask for a postponement once
three players are away. The rearranged dates stretch as far as 15 December.
Until then the table is a list of games in hand.

**Run over.** Plymouth 0–1 Burton. Three straight wins ended in front of
15,372 at Home Park, to a Jack Armer goal on 70 minutes. Burton had won one of
their previous four.

**Late, then later.** Wycombe 2–2 Reading. Paudie O'Connor put Reading ahead
two minutes after the restart, and Jayden Wareham equalised. Jacob Brown
restored the lead with ten minutes left, and Danny Ings levelled again on 90
minutes.

**Elsewhere.** Cambridge trailed AFC Wimbledon to a Jayden Stockley goal until
Zak Bradshaw equalised in the first minute of stoppage time. Stockport beat
Peterborough 1–0 with a Kyle Wootton goal six minutes into the second half.

**No coupon.** No League One fixture was on the coupon, so there is nothing to
score.
