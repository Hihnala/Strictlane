---
title: Premier League MW6
summary: City won 3–0 at Anfield, Coventry won a top-flight home game for the first time since 2001, and Ipswich scored twice in the last three minutes.
---
**Statement result.** Liverpool 0–3 Manchester City. Level for an hour, then
Ryan Gravenberch took a second yellow for a challenge on Jérémy Doku in the
62nd minute, a call The Analyst called harsh, and City scored three
in 16 minutes. Erling Haaland and Doku scored within three minutes, and Rayan Cherki ended a 41-pass move, the longest build-up to a
Premier League goal since September 2023. Haaland has now scored in eight
straight games against Liverpool. City are three points clear, and Anfield
has its joint-heaviest Premier League home defeat.

**Biggest surprise.** Coventry 4–1 Newcastle. Coventry's first top-flight home
win since April 2001, and it started with a gift: Lukas Hornicek spilled a
routine ball, brought down Sidiki Cherif, and Jack Rudoni scored the penalty.
Cherif, Ellis Simms and the substitute Stephen Mfuni added the rest. Sven
Botman's strike on 70 minutes was all Newcastle had in reply.

**The soft spot.** Ipswich 2–1 Fulham. Ryan Sessegnon's 25-yard strike put
Fulham ahead on 55 minutes, and for half an hour that was the afternoon. Then
Abdoul Ouattara scored from 30 yards in the 88th and Dara O'Shea headed in
Jack Clarke's free kick a minute into stoppage time. Ipswich are ninth. Fulham
have two points from six games.

**The favourite's reply.** Arsenal 2–1 Leeds. A flat first half, then James
Justin put Leeds ahead on 55 minutes. Arteta sent on Bruno Guimarães, Jurrien
Timber and Viktor Gyökeres, Riccardo Calafiori headed the equaliser seven
minutes later, and Guimarães won it from the edge of the box. A better answer
to Brighton than the first 55 minutes suggested was coming.

**Worst decision.** Rodrigo Bentancur headbutted Luke Shaw shortly after Bryan
Mbeumo had put United ahead, and went off with a straight red. United then
spent the rest of the match failing to beat ten men, and Dominic Solanke
headed Spurs level in stoppage time.

**Elsewhere.** Jordan Henderson, 36, scored twice and set up another as
Chelsea beat Bournemouth 5–1, two up inside three minutes. Oliver Glasner's
return to Selhurst Park ended in a 3–1 Forest defeat. Brighton won 2–0 at Sunderland to stay in the top four, Hull kept
their home record intact with a 2–2 against Everton, and Villa and Brentford
also shared four goals.
