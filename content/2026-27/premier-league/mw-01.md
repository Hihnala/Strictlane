---
title: Premier League MW1
summary: Hull beat Manchester United. Brentford dismantle a Spurs defence held together with tape.
---
**Biggest surprise.** Hull City 2–0 Manchester United. A promoted side, at home,
against a club that has now made "difficult opening fixture" a permanent feature
of its identity rather than an occasional misfortune.

**Statement result.** Brentford 3–0 Tottenham. Spurs arrived without Van de Ven,
Porro and Sarr — captain, vice-captain and the midfielder who covers for both —
and set up as though nobody had circulated the memo. Archie Gray at right-back
against Brentford's press was less a plan than a hostage situation. Three down
before the hour.

**Quietly ruthless.** Arsenal 3–0 Coventry. Efficient, unbothered, done by the
hour, and let's be honest about what it was: a promoted side away at the
champions on opening night. Beating Coventry 3–0 tells us the machine starts.
It tells us nothing about September.

**Soft spot, hard look.** Ipswich 2–1 Sunderland, and Nilson Angulo's free kick
went in off the underside of the bar past a goalkeeper who is six foot nine,
which is the kind of thing that stays with you. The performance underneath it was
scrappier than the scoreline — Sunderland had the better of the second half and
found nothing — but new manager, opening day, three points. Take it and say
nothing clever.

**What I got wrong.** Three of four. Forest 0–1 Leeds was the miss, and it was a
coin-flip fixture I'd marked 1X on a 41/30/29 split. Leeds have an oddly strong
opening-day record and I noted it, then didn't act on it. That's not variance,
that's reading the evidence and shrugging.
