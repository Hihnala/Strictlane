---
title: Premier League MW1
summary: Hull beat Manchester United, Brentford take Spurs apart, and Ipswich win it in the 90th minute.
---
**Biggest surprise.** Hull City 2–0 Manchester United. A promoted side, back in
the Premier League after nine years, against a club that has now made
"difficult opening fixture" a permanent feature of its identity rather than an
occasional misfortune. Semi Ajayi and Nobel Mendy both scored from set pieces
before half-time.

**Statement result.** Brentford 3–0 Tottenham. Keane Lewis-Potter, Vitaly
Janelt and Michael Kayode scored, Igor Thiago hit the post with a penalty, and
Spurs didn't manage a shot on target before half-time. Archie Gray, 20, became
the club's youngest captain in a competitive match. Not a record anyone at
Spurs will frame. The defeat equalled their heaviest opening-day loss of the
Premier League era.

**Quietly ruthless.** Arsenal 3–0 Coventry. Havertz and Saka scored inside 23
minutes, Ødegaard added the third four minutes after the break, and that was
that. It was a promoted side away at the champions on opening night. Beating
Coventry 3–0 tells us the machine starts. It tells us nothing about September.

**Soft spot, hard look.** Ipswich 2–1 Sunderland. Emersonn scored on 24
minutes, Nilson Angulo equalised with a free kick that went in off the bar,
and Jack Clarke, once of Sunderland, came off the bench to curl in the winner
on 90. Sunderland had more of the ball and few chances to show for it. Opening
day, three points. Take it and say nothing clever.

**What I got wrong.** Three of four. Forest 0–1 Leeds was the miss, a
coin-flip fixture where I'd left the away win out. Anton Stach's free kick on
87 minutes gave Leeds their first win over Forest since 1970. Leeds have an
oddly strong opening-day record and I noted it, then didn't act on it. That's
not variance, that's reading the evidence and shrugging.
