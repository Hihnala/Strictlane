---
title: Premier League MW2
summary: Chelsea win a seven-goal game on a quarter of the ball, Villa go two matches without a goal, and both our singles missed.
---
**Biggest surprise.** Chelsea 4–3 Brighton. Brighton arrived off a 4–0 win
over Villa and lost a seven-goal game to a side that had about a quarter of the
ball and completed 152 passes, Chelsea's fewest since Opta records began in
2003-04. Lavia, Neto and João Pedro were among the scorers, and Chelsea led
3–1 at half-time. Ruthless on the counter and a defence that conceded three at
home. Both of those are true, and only one of them survives a harder fixture.

**Worst afternoon.** Aston Villa. Two matches, no goals, and the number that
actually indicts them: not one shot on target against Arsenal. Buendía hit the
crossbar from 22 yards early on and that was the whole of Villa's evening.

**Arsenal, since I have to.** Saka finished from close range just before the
hour, turning in Calafiori's cross after the half-time substitute Eze had
found him. Arsenal were nowhere near the level of the Coventry night. Long
spells of possession, one goal. Winning badly is a useful habit. It is not the
same as being good, and saying so is the entire point of writing any of this
down.

**Ipswich.** Led at Old Trafford through Leif Davis on 29 minutes, lost 5–2.
Bruno Fernandes scored a hat-trick, one of them a penalty, and two of United's
goals came off deflections. The scoreline flattered United, which changes
nothing about the scoreline.

**What I got wrong.** Both singles, both in this league. Bournemouth led
Everton at half-time and finished 1–1. Coventry lost at home to Hull, and that
was the one I'd doubted in advance. The doubt was argued and written down. I
still played it as a single, and it was wrong.
