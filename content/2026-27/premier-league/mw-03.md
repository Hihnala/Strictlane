title: Premier League MW3
summary: Arsenal come from behind to beat Chelsea, Fulham lose a match they led at half-time, and two coupon draws go unmarked.
---
**Statement result.** Arsenal 2–1 Chelsea. Declared bias, so the bad bit first:
the champions conceded after 77 seconds because nobody cleared a Reece James
free-kick, and Morgan Rogers volleyed it in. After that it was Arsenal's match.
Havertz equalised against his old club on 25 minutes, and Ødegaard finished
five minutes after the break once Havertz had dummied the ball. Raya still had
to make a late save from Estêvão. Three from three, level on points with City.

**Biggest surprise.** Fulham 2–3 Crystal Palace. Josh King and César Palacios
had Fulham 2–1 up at half-time. Then Palace's full-backs took over: Tyrick
Mitchell scored twice and Ben Chilwell got the winner. All three Palace goals
came from defenders who play on the touchline, which says nothing good about
Fulham.

**Worst defence.** Manchester United have conceded twice in each of their
first three league games. At Everton they led twice, through Mbeumo a minute
into the second half and Šeško on 88 after Tyrique George had levelled, and
still drew. Ainsley Maitland-Niles came off the bench on his Everton debut and
curled a 25-yard shot into the top corner six minutes into stoppage time.

**Ipswich.** Liverpool's first win of the season came at Portman Road on
Friday, and the first nine minutes were the match: Isak scored on six and nine,
both from Gakpo passes. Ipswich had more shots, 14 to 10, and none of that
counts once you've shipped two before the tenth minute.

**What I got wrong.** Two draws landed off the coupon. Forest 0–0 Tottenham was
the most evenly balanced match on the card and I left the draw out anyway.
Brentford 1–1 Sunderland went in as a single, and Sunderland were the better
side: they dominated the first half, Le Fée hit the post and Dan Ballard had a
goal ruled out by VAR before Le Fée levelled from the spot with about ten
minutes left. That was bad reasoning, not bad luck. Hull 0–0 Aston Villa went
the other way, and Hull have seven points from three.
