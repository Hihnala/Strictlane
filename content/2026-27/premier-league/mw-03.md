---
title: Premier League MW3
summary: Arsenal come from behind to beat Chelsea, Fulham lose a match they dominated, and two coupon draws go unmarked.
---
**Statement result.** Arsenal 2–1 Chelsea. Declared bias, so the bad bit first:
the champions conceded after 77 seconds because nobody cleared a Reece James
free-kick, and Morgan Rogers volleyed it in. After that it was Arsenal's match.
Havertz equalised against his old club on 25 minutes, Ødegaard finished a
flowing move five minutes after the break, and a Chelsea side that had scored
early in every game so far finished on 0.39 xG. Raya still had to make a late
save from Estêvão. Three from three, level with City.

**Biggest surprise.** Fulham 2–3 Crystal Palace. Fulham had 25 shots to 10,
3.14 xG to 1.97, and a 2–1 lead at half-time. Mitchell scored twice and
Chilwell added the third on 77 minutes. Fulham
did enough to win and finished like a side that didn't.

**Worst decision.** Manchester United didn't sign a defender, and have now
conceded twice in each of their first three league games. At Everton they led
twice, through Mbeumo a minute into the second half and a Sesko header on 88,
and still drew, because Ainsley Maitland-Niles, signed on the Tuesday, came off
the bench and put a 30-yard shot into the top corner in the 96th minute.

**Ipswich.** Liverpool's first win of the season came at Portman Road on
Friday, and the first nine minutes were the match: Isak scored on six and nine,
both from Gakpo passes. Ipswich had more shots and near-identical xG, and none
of that counts once you've shipped two before the tenth minute.

**What I got wrong.** Two draws landed off the coupon. Forest 0–0 Tottenham was
the most evenly priced match on the card and I left the draw out anyway; Spurs
didn't manage a shot on target. Brentford 1–1 Sunderland went in as a single,
and Sunderland had the better xG and five shots on target to two before Le Fée's
82nd-minute penalty. That was bad reasoning, not bad luck. Hull 0–0 Aston Villa
went the other way: Villa built nearly two xG, put one shot on target, and Hull
have seven points from three.
