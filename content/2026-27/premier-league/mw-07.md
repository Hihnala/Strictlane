---
title: Premier League MW7
summary: City made it seven wins from seven, nine-man Brentford lost their unbeaten record to Liverpool, and Tottenham finally won a league game.
---
**Seven from seven.** Manchester City 3–2 Ipswich. Phil Foden scored on 19
minutes, Daizen Maeda equalised two minutes later, and Erling Haaland scored
on 34 and 69. Zian Flemming's penalty on 90 flattered Ipswich, who had 8 shots
to City's 19. City have 21 points out of 21. Arsenal won 1–0 at Forest and
stay three behind.

**Ten minutes.** Brighton 5–0 Crystal Palace. Jaydee Canvot turned the ball
into his own net on four minutes, Diego Gomez scored on seven and Yasin Ayari
on ten. Charalampos Kostoulas and Jack Hinshelwood added the rest. Palace had
one shot on target all afternoon. Brighton have won four in a row and their 23
goals are the most in the league.

**Nine men.** Brentford 1–2 Liverpool. Keane Lewis-Potter headed Brentford
ahead on 44 minutes. In first-half stoppage time Yehor Yarmolyuk went off with
a straight red for a headbutting action at Jeremy Jacquet. Victor Muñoz
equalised on 62, and Alexander Isak, back from injury, won it on 70. Vitaly
Janelt's second yellow came in stoppage time.

**At last.** Tottenham 3–1 Coventry, on Monday. James Maddison scored on 14
minutes, Jack Rudoni equalised on 50, Omar Marmoush restored the lead on 58
and Ben Davies added a third six minutes into stoppage time. It was Spurs'
first league win of the season, at the seventh attempt. Sunderland are now
bottom on four points.

**Elsewhere.** Brentford and Everton both went into the round unbeaten. Both
lost, Everton 2–0 at home to Chelsea. Alex Iwobi's goal beat Hull and gave
Fulham their first win, and Bournemouth got theirs with a 2–0 against
Sunderland, who have lost four in a row. Aston Villa won 1–0 at Newcastle.
Dominic Calvert-Lewin cancelled out Bryan Mbeumo's opener in a 1–1 at Elland
Road.

**What I got wrong.** Three of four. Brentford went in as a single and led at
half-time, then played the whole second half a man down and lost. Fulham, City
and Villa's win at Newcastle were all covered.
