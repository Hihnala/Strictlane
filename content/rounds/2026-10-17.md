---
title: Round review — 17 October 2026
summary: Ten from thirteen, level with the season's best. Seven of eight hedges landed, and the round's only draw came at Southampton.
---
Ten from thirteen. That equals the 5 September round, and nothing this season
has done better.

The hedges carried it. Seven of eight landed, most of them with the draw left
out, and the round produced exactly one draw. Six matches went to the away
side, and three of those fell inside a hedge: Aston Villa at Newcastle, Stoke
at Derby and Sheffield United at Bristol City, all 1–0. That's two away wins
in two weeks for Stoke, and the coupon had both of them.

The one draw landed in the one place that couldn't take it. Southampton were
the pool's clear favourites against Bolton and drew 0–0. Home and away were
both covered. The draw wasn't.

Three of the five singles came in. West Ham won 1–0 at Swansea, Birmingham
beat QPR 3–0 and Fulham edged Hull 1–0. Brentford was the only single that
went against the pool, and Liverpool won 2–1. Last week's note about backing
against the crowd got its follow-up within seven days. Norwich losing 0–1 at
home to Portsmouth was the plainer miss: a home favourite in everyone's eyes,
the coupon's included, that couldn't score.

Ipswich scored twice at the Etihad and still lost 3–2. The draw was on the
coupon as cover, City won anyway, and the line held. Two goals away at City
is not nothing, but it's also no points.

The big scores came at Hillsborough and Wrexham. Sheffield Wednesday beat AFC
Wimbledon 5–0 and Wrexham beat Preston 4–1, with West Brom squeezing past
Blackburn 3–2. All three were hedges and all three landed.

Note for the model. Leaving the draw out paid this week because there was only
one of them. The week before there were four. Two rounds say nothing about the
draw rate, so the split stays as it is until there's a longer run to judge it
by.
