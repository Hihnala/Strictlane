Seven from thirteen. The hedges did their job and the singles didn't.

Six draws, and this time the coupon was ready for most of them. Burnley and
Derby, Portsmouth and Blackburn, Oxford and Cambridge, and Sheffield Wednesday
and Stockport all finished level with the draw covered. Birmingham 2–2
Middlesbrough and QPR 2–2 Preston had it left out. Six of the eight hedges
landed in all, with Swansea winning 2–1 at Lincoln and Bradford 1–0 at Luton.

Only one single came in: Everton 1–0 Ipswich. Nottingham Forest lost 1–0 at
home to Coventry, and Southampton lost 2–1 at Wrexham. Hull were backed to win
at Newcastle against a pool that had Newcastle as favourites, and Newcastle
won 2–1. QPR's home win went to a draw.

Brighton 3–0 Arsenal was the clearest miss on the card. A Brighton win had
been ruled out entirely. Arsenal are my team, but this one can't be put down
to bias: the coupon rated Arsenal lower than the pool did. It was still wrong,
and by a wider margin than anything else this week.

Note for the model. Where the draw was covered it came in four times. Of the
five singles, one landed. Under 8+0 the singles carry the most risk, and this
round showed it.
