title: Round review — 21 November 2026
summary: Eight from thirteen, two short of the season's best. Two of the five singles came in and six of the eight hedges held.
---
Eight from thirteen. That ends a run of two tens, two short of the season's
best. It's the season's fourth eight, after 12 September, 24 October and 31
October. The hedges went six from eight. The singles went two from five, and
that's where the round was lost.

**What I got wrong.** The singles went on the wrong matches. Two weeks after the
7 November note said a single belongs on one of the clearest favourites, four of
the five went on four of the five most open matches in the pool: Swansea, Stoke,
Coventry and Bolton. Only Coventry came in, 3–2 against Crystal Palace after 2–2
at the break. Stoke beat Birmingham 1–0 with Birmingham backed alone. Swansea
drew 2–2 with West Brom and Bolton drew 1–1 with Portsmouth. Meanwhile
Tottenham, Sheffield United, Arsenal and Aston Villa had big pool majorities and
got hedges. The five firmest pool favourites on the coupon were Wolves,
Middlesbrough, Sheffield United, Tottenham and Arsenal, and four of them won.
This miss was in the build. Variance gets no share of it.

Swansea was also the first single against the pool's pick since 24 October, by a
hair. On Wednesday the pool had Swansea in front. By Saturday morning West Brom
had edged ahead. Nobody decided to go against the crowd, so nobody wrote a
reason down. It drew, so neither reading won.

The hedges did their job where it was hard. Chelsea were 1–1 with Leeds at
half-time and lost 1–4, a clear home favourite beaten with the away win covered.
Lincoln lost 0–1 to Wrexham with the draw and the away win in. The two hedges
that broke both left out the draw. Aston Villa drew 0–0 with Sunderland. Arsenal
led 1–0 at half-time at Newcastle and drew 2–2, with a hedge that covered either
side winning. The pool had Arsenal clearly, and Arsenal didn't deliver. Ipswich
lost 2–0 at Tottenham, as the pool had it.

Seven home wins, four draws and two away wins. 35 goals. Four draws is the most
since 10 October and none of them was covered, which makes fifteen draws in six
rounds without one in the marks. The draw went on twice. One was Wolves, the
firmest favourite on the coupon, which is the upset cover the 31 October note
told me to stop using. Wolves won 2–1.

Note for the model. Turn the 7 November note into a check. Rank the thirteen by
the size of the pool's favourite before any marks go in, and take the singles
from the top of that list unless the reason is written down. Swansea was the
closest home and away split on the coupon, exactly the match the 31 October note
wants the draw on, and it went in as a single and drew. Run the against-the-pool
check on Saturday's pool, since the crowd can move after Wednesday. Singles are
now 31 from 60 this season, hedges 71 from 96.
