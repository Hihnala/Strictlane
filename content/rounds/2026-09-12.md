Eight from thirteen, and the draws decided it again.

Five matches finished level and the coupon was ready for one of them:
Tottenham 0–0 Everton, hedged with the draw included. The other four all
landed where the draw had been left out. Liverpool 0–0 Fulham and Chelsea 2–2
Hull were home singles. Bournemouth 2–2 Brentford and Charlton 0–0 Portsmouth
were hedges on either side winning. That's three rounds running where the
excluded draw has been the miss.

Three of the five singles landed: Arsenal won 2–0 at Sunderland, Southampton
beat Bristol City 4–1 and Middlesbrough beat Norwich 4–3. The hedges caught
three upsets, with Nottingham Forest winning 2–1 at Villa Park, Stoke 2–1 at
Watford and Blackburn beating Millwall 3–1.

Ipswich won 3–2 at Crystal Palace, and the away win was exactly the outcome
the coupon had ruled out. A miss, but at least the soft spot stayed out of
the forecast.

Note for the model. An 8+0 system has to drop a sign somewhere, and for three
rounds it has mostly been the draw. When the same sign fails week after week,
bad luck stops being an excuse.
