title: Round review — 29 August 2026
summary: Nine from thirteen. The hedges carried the round, and all three draws landed on matches where the draw had been left out.
---
Nine from thirteen, a good afternoon for the coupon that the forecasting can
take limited credit for.

Seven of the eight hedges landed, and they were the round. Three home sides
the pool had as favourites lost, and all three were on two signs. Newcastle
won 2–0 at Tottenham, QPR won 2–1 at Blackburn and Lincoln won 1–0 at Bolton.
Norwich were 3–1 up on Burnley by half-time and won 4–1. Huddersfield won 2–1
at Cambridge, and Bristol City beat Portsmouth by the same score.

Stockport beat Wycombe 5–1 and the line held. The forecast behind it was a
hand estimate read off form and the venue, with nothing firmer underneath. A
guess that lands is still a guess.

Only two of the five singles came in: Southampton thrashed Millwall 5–1 and
Charlton edged Preston 1–0. Coventry lost 1–0 at home to Hull. Bournemouth led
Everton at half-time and drew 1–1. Watford and West Ham drew by the same
score, with West Ham backed alone.

All three draws landed on matches where the draw wasn't covered. Alongside
Bournemouth and Watford, Cardiff and Sheffield United drew 2–2 with every goal
in the first half. That's two rounds running where the excluded draw has been
the miss. The number of draws wasn't unusual. The habit of dropping the draw
to make the coupon fit the system was the expensive part.

Note for the model. The forecast went in on Tuesday, four days before
kickoff, working from four-day-old team news. Blackburn and QPR is exactly the
kind of match where that gap matters: the forecast leaned home, QPR won, and
only the hedge saved the line.
