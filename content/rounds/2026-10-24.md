---
title: Round review — 24 October 2026
summary: Eight from thirteen, two short of the season's best. Six of eight hedges landed, but only two of the five singles came in.
---
Eight from thirteen. That's two behind the season's best (5 September and 17
October) and level with 12 September.

All eight hedges had the same shape: home and away covered, the draw left
out. A hedge built like that can only fail one way, and it failed twice.
Preston drew 1–1 with West Brom and Stoke drew 0–0 with Bristol City. The
other six held, including the two messiest matches of the round. West Ham were
1–3 down to Southampton at half-time and lost 2–3. Cambridge trailed Plymouth
at the break and lost by the same score. Arsenal beat Everton 2–0 and
Tottenham won 2–0 at Chelsea, both on the same setup.

The singles went two from five. Fulham won 1–0 at Coventry, and QPR beat
Swansea 5–0 after leading 4–0 at the interval. QPR was a call against the
pool, which had Swansea as favourites. That one paid.

The other three lost. Blackburn beat Wrexham 2–0 with Wrexham backed alone.
Oxford and Bradford drew 0–0 with Bradford backed alone. Both of those went
against the pool too. Lincoln beat Burnley 4–1 with Burnley backed alone, and
this time the crowd had agreed with the coupon.

**What I got wrong.** Lincoln–Burnley was the costliest miss. It was 1–1 at
half-time and Lincoln scored three after it. No cover and the crowd on the
same side, so there's nobody else to share it with. Two weeks ago Burnley were
left off the coupon entirely and won 4–0 at Watford. This week they were the
only mark and lost 4–1. The coupon has now been wrong about Burnley by the same
margin in both directions.

Three draws in thirteen, after four on 10 October and one on 17 October. Six
home wins and four away wins. 31 goals, just under two and a half a match.
None of the three draws was covered. Two of them broke hedges and the third
beat a single.

Note for the model. Three singles went against the pool and one of them came
in. The 10 October note asked for a written reason before kickoff whenever the
coupon goes against the crowd, and none of these three has one. The draw was
also left out of all eight hedges. That's eight draws across the last three
rounds, so at least one hedge per coupon should carry the draw until the run
says otherwise.
