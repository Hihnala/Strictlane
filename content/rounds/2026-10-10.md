---
title: Round review — 10 October 2026
summary: Seven from thirteen. Watford–Burnley was the costliest miss, and two outright calls lost to draws again.
---
Seven from thirteen, and the misses sorted themselves into two neat piles.

The first pile is familiar. Three of the five outright calls landed. Chelsea
took Bournemouth apart 5–1 and were two up at the break. Brighton won 2–0 at
Sunderland with both goals after half-time, and Stoke won 2–1 at Bolton after
leading at the interval. The other two lost to the draw. Manchester United and
Tottenham finished 1–1 with no draw on the coupon, and Aston Villa 2–2
Brentford came in with Brentford backed alone. The 19 September lesson
repeated word for word: the outright call loses to the result it refused to
cover.

The second pile cost more. Four of the eight hedges landed (Ipswich,
Preston–Millwall, Blackburn and Derby–Wrexham), and in the other four the
excluded outcome was exactly the one that happened. Middlesbrough beat Wolves
1–0 with the home win ruled out. Sheffield United beat Lincoln 3–0 on the same
setup. Lincoln had been read as the stronger side away from home, and Bramall
Lane corrected that with three goals.

Watford–Burnley gets its own paragraph. Burnley were left off the coupon
entirely and led 3–0 at half-time. The 0–4 was the round's biggest miss, and
nothing in the match pointed to bad luck. Wrong read, and a defeat too clear
to need explaining.

Huddersfield–Sheffield Wednesday was the fourth. The draw was excluded, it was
0–0 at the break and 2–2 at the end. The coupon leaned towards Wednesday even
though the Vakio pool had the hosts as clear favourites. This time neither of
them won.

Ipswich beat Fulham 2–1 with both home goals after half-time. The hedge
covered a win or a draw and Ipswich picked the better one. Soft spot intact,
and nothing to be embarrassed about this week.

Note for the model. Two of the lost hedges ruled out a home win, and in both
the pool had leaned towards the hosts. Going against the crowd is fine, but
the reason needs to be written down before kickoff. Four draws in thirteen,
and one of them covered.
