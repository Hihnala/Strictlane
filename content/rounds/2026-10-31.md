title: Round review — 31 October 2026
summary: Eight from thirteen, two short of the season's best. Four of the five singles came in, but only four of the eight hedges held.
---
Eight from thirteen, the same as last week and two behind the season's best
(5 September and 17 October). It's the season's third eight, after 12 September
and 24 October. The route was the reverse of last week: singles four from five,
hedges four from eight.

All five singles sided with the pool's favourite. Tottenham beat Crystal Palace
2–0, Middlesbrough beat Charlton 3–0 and Wolves beat Cardiff 3–0. Swansea,
beaten 5–0 at QPR a week ago, beat Blackburn 4–0. Aston Villa were the one that
slipped. They led Fulham at half-time and drew 1–1. A home favourite the crowd
agreed on, held to a draw: that's ordinary variance, and I'd back Villa alone
again.

**What I got wrong.** The draw. The 24 October note asked for at least one
hedge per coupon to carry it, and this coupon had three: Coventry, West Ham and
Southampton. None of them needed it. Coventry beat Sunderland 3–1 in the only
hedge that left out the home win. West Ham lost 1–3 at home to West Brom. Last
week the coupon dropped the draw and kept the away win, and West Ham lost. This
week it swapped the away win for the draw, and West Ham lost again.

The three draws landed where the draw had been left out. Hull drew 2–2
with Ipswich, Burnley drew 0–0 with Portsmouth and Villa was a single. That's
two rounds running with three draws and not one of them covered.

Hull–Ipswich is the one I'd change. Ipswich led at the break and finished
level. The pool rated the draw ahead of an Ipswich win, but the coupon kept
Ipswich and dropped the draw. The forecast had those two the other way round,
so the numbers made that call. With a declared soft spot for Ipswich, I'd rather
say that here myself.

Burnley have now beaten the coupon three times with three different results.
On 10 October they won at Watford with the away win ruled out. On 24 October
they lost at Lincoln as a single. Now a 0–0 with the draw left out.

The best read was Manchester City. The pool had City as clear favourites, and
the hedge kept Brighton in and left out the draw. Brighton led at half-time and
won 1–0 at the Etihad.

Eight home wins, three draws and two away wins. 36 goals, five more than last
week.

Note for the model. Keep the draw in the hedges, but put it in the right ones.
This week it went on two home favourites as cover against an upset, and the
draws turned up in the matches the coupon read as open: Hull–Ipswich and
Burnley–Portsmouth. A draw cover belongs on a match where home and away are
close. Six draws in two
rounds and none covered says the placement is wrong. No single went against the
pool this week, so the 10 October rule about writing down a reason had nothing
to test.
